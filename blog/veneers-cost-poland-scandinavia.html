Veneer prices in Poland and the Nordic countries (2026)

What porcelain and composite veneers cost per tooth in Poland and in the Nordic countries, and what to check before you compare.

Veneers are thin shells, usually porcelain, bonded to the front of the teeth. They are not covered by public dental support in any of the Nordic countries, so you pay the full price wherever you are treated. Below are typical prices per tooth from public price lists in November 2026. The clinic sets the final price after an examination. If you are still deciding between treatments, read veneers, crowns or whitening.

Porcelain or composite?

Porcelain keeps its colour and shine for many years and usually lasts ten years or more. Composite is built up directly on the tooth, costs less and can be repaired, but it stains and wears faster. Your dentist recommends a material after an examination.

When does Poland pay off?

Because most people get six to ten veneers, the difference per tooth adds up. Compared with Finland and with clinics in Stockholm, the saving is often clear. In Norway and Denmark, the cheapest local clinics are close to Polish prices, so compare written quotes for the same number of teeth and the same material. Remember travel and accommodation, usually for one stay, and our concierge fee, which is always shown separately.

What to check in a quote

Ask whether the price includes the examination, the smile design or mock-up, temporary veneers, the final veneers and a check-up. Ask which laboratory makes them and what the guarantee covers. Common questions

How much does one porcelain veneer cost in Poland?

In Gdańsk, a porcelain veneer typically costs about 2,000 to 2,800 PLN per tooth in 2026, about 450 to 630 EUR. Composite veneers cost about 600 to 1,000 PLN.

How many veneers do people usually get?

Often six to ten teeth in the upper jaw, the ones that show when you smile. The dentist recommends the number after an examination and a smile design.

How long does a veneer treatment take?

Porcelain veneers usually need two visits about a week apart: preparation and impressions first, then fitting. Many clients plan one stay of seven to ten days.
