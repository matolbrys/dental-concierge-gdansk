Dental implant prices: Poland compared with Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland (2026)

What a dental implant with crown costs in Poland and in the Nordic countries, what is not included, and when it pays off.

Price is the main reason many people from the Nordic countries look at dental treatment in Poland. Below are typical prices for one implant with abutment and crown in each country, based on public price lists in November 2026. They are a guide only: the clinic sets the final price after an examination and a 3D scan.

Prices at a glance

What is included

The prices above usually include the implant, the surgery, the abutment and the final crown. Scans, temporary teeth, a bone graft or a sinus lift are often charged separately. A clear quote lists each of these, so you can compare offers line by line.

What is not included

Implant treatment usually takes two trips a few months apart. Add flights and accommodation for both stays, time off work, and our concierge fee, which we always show separately from the clinic's costs before you commit.

When does treatment in Poland pay off?

For a single implant, the saving after travel can be modest, especially compared with lower-priced clinics at home. The difference grows with several implants, bridges or a full arch, where the treatment cost is much higher than the travel cost. In Sweden, the state subsidy can make a single implant as cheap at home, so it is worth getting a Swedish quote too.

Why are prices lower in Poland?

Mainly because salaries and running costs are lower. Many Polish clinics use the same international implant systems as clinics in the Nordic countries. Ask which system and which laboratory will be used, so you can compare like with like.

How to compare offers

Ask for a written plan with the price of every step, the implant system, the guarantee and how follow-up works. Our 8 questions to ask before dental implants abroad are a good checklist.

Common questions

How much does a dental implant cost in Poland?

At clinics in Gdańsk, a single implant with abutment and crown typically costs 6,500 to 8,500 PLN in 2026, about 1,450 to 1,900 EUR. A bone graft, if needed, is extra.

Is a dental implant cheaper in Poland than in Scandinavia?

Usually yes in Norway, Denmark and Finland, where adults pay for implants themselves. In Sweden the state dental subsidy can make a single implant as cheap at home, so it is worth comparing both.

What is not included in the price?

Travel and accommodation for usually two trips, any bone graft or sinus lift, and our concierge fee, which is always shown separately in your quote.
