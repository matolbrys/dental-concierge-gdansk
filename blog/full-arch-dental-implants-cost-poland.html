All-on-4 and full-arch implants: prices in Poland and the Nordic countries (2026)

What a full arch of fixed teeth on four to six implants costs in Poland and in the Nordic countries, and what to compare.

When all the teeth in a jaw are missing or failing, a fixed bridge on four to six implants can replace them. This is often called All-on-4 or All-on-6. It is the treatment where prices differ most between countries, and also where offers are hardest to compare. Below are typical prices from public price lists in November 2026. The clinic sets the final price after an examination and a 3D scan.

What is full-arch treatment?

The dentist places four to six implants in one jaw. In many cases a temporary bridge is attached the same day, so you leave with fixed teeth. After three to six months of healing, the final bridge is made, in acrylic, composite or zirconia. Read more about the usual course in implant treatment step by step.

What affects the price

The implant system, the number of implants, the material of the final bridge and whether you need extractions or bone grafting make the biggest difference. In Poland, a complete treatment with a final zirconia bridge from a premium implant system is usually at the top of the range. Always check whether a quote includes the final bridge or only the surgery and temporary teeth.

When does Poland pay off?

In Denmark and Finland, where adults pay for implants themselves, the difference for a full arch is often large. In Norway, prices vary so much between clinics that it is worth comparing a written quote from home with one from Gdańsk. In Sweden, the state subsidy covers most of the cost above a certain level, so a Swedish quote with the subsidy deducted is often hard to beat. Remember travel and accommodation for usually two or three stays, and our concierge fee, which is always shown separately.

How to compare offers

Ask for a written plan that names the implant system, the number of implants, the material of the temporary and final bridge, every visit and what the guarantee covers. Common questions

How much does All-on-4 cost in Poland?

A complete treatment for one jaw, with implants, a temporary bridge and the final bridge, typically costs about 30,000 to 65,000 PLN in 2026, about 6,700 to 14,600 EUR, depending on the implant system and bridge material.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6?

Both give a fixed bridge on implants for a whole jaw. All-on-4 uses four implants and All-on-6 uses six, which spreads the load more. Your dentist recommends one after a 3D scan.

How many trips does full-arch treatment need?

Usually two or three. The implants and a temporary bridge are often placed during the first stay, and the final bridge is fitted after three to six months of healing.
