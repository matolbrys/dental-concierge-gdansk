Dental implant treatment step by step: why it usually takes two trips

How dental implant treatment works, from the first scan to the final crown, and why it usually takes two trips.

Your dentist sets the final plan, and every case is different. Most dental implant treatments still follow the same outline, and knowing it makes it easier to plan your travel and your time off work.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium or zirconia screw that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once it has fused with the jawbone, it carries a crown, a bridge or a full set of teeth. An implant does not rely on the neighbouring teeth, so they do not need to be ground down as they would for a traditional bridge.

Who are dental implants suitable for?

Implants can suit most adults who have lost one or more teeth, as long as the gums are healthy and there is enough bone to hold the implant. Smoking, diabetes that is not well controlled and some medicines can affect healing, so the clinic will ask about your health before planning anything. If there is not enough bone, a bone graft or sinus lift may be needed first, which adds time to the treatment.

Single implants, bridges and full arches

One implant can replace one tooth. Two or more implants can carry a bridge when several teeth in a row are missing. When all the teeth in a jaw are missing, four to six implants can hold a fixed full arch, often called All-on-4 or All-on-6. Your dentist recommends the solution after the examination and the 3D scan.

Before you travel

Recent X-rays or photos give the clinic a first idea of whether implants are an option for you. The decision itself is made in person, after an examination. Ask for a written plan with the expected number of visits and a price for each step.

First trip: examination, 3D scan and placement

The clinic examines you and takes a 3D scan (CBCT) to measure the bone and plan where each implant goes. If everything looks right, the implant can often be placed during the same stay, usually under local anaesthesia. If the gap shows when you smile, you go home with a temporary tooth. Plan for about four to seven days in Gdańsk, so the clinic can check the healing before you fly home.

Healing at home

After placement, bone grows onto the surface of the implant. This is called osseointegration and usually takes three to six months, longer if you needed a bone graft. You spend this time at home and live normally, although you need to be a little careful when eating. Some swelling and tenderness in the first days is expected. Our article on dental implant complications explains what is normal and which signs need a dentist.

Second trip: the final crown

Once the implant has healed, the clinic fits the abutment and takes an impression or a digital scan. A dental laboratory makes the final crown or bridge, which is often fitted within a few days. Plan for about three to five days in Gdańsk. You leave this visit with your final teeth.

If you need a bone graft, healing can take longer, and an extra visit is sometimes needed.

Are there faster options?

Sometimes a temporary tooth can be attached to the implant on the day of surgery. This is called immediate loading. It depends on the quality of your bone and on how stable the implant is, and only your dentist can decide it after the scan. Even then, the final crown is usually made after healing, so a second trip is still common.

How long do dental implants last?

With good cleaning and regular check-ups, implants often last for many years, and for many people the rest of their life. The crown on top wears like a natural tooth and may need replacing after ten to fifteen years. Smoking and gum inflammation are among the most common reasons implants fail over time.

What affects the price?

The price depends on the number of implants, the implant system, whether you need a bone graft or sinus lift, and the material of the crown. A clear quote lists each of these separately. After treatment

Clean your implants the way you clean your natural teeth, including between them, and go for regular check-ups. Your dentist at home can do these if you bring a record of the implant system that was used.

We plan both trips with you and stay in touch with the clinic between your visits. Every medical decision stays with your dentist.

Common questions

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Usually three to six months from placement to the final crown, because the bone needs time to heal around the implant. A bone graft can make it longer.

How many trips to Poland do I need for dental implants?

Most patients travel twice: once for the examination and placement, and once for the final crown three to six months later. Your dentist confirms this after the 3D scan.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The implant is usually placed under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Tenderness and swelling for a few days afterwards are common, and the clinic tells you how to manage them.

Can I get new teeth on the same day as the implant?

Sometimes. A temporary tooth can be attached on the day of surgery if the bone and the implant are stable enough. Only your dentist can decide this after the scan.

This article is general information and not medical advice. Your treating dentist will advise you on the treatment that is right for you.
